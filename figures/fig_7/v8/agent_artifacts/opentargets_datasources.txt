GENETIC ASSOCIATION
-------------------
gwas_credible_sets
    GWAS fine-mapped credible sets from large studies (FinnGen, UK Biobank, GBMI, etc.).
    Fine-mapping identifies the most probable causal variants at each GWAS locus.

eva
    ClinVar / European Variation Archive (EVA) — germline variant-disease associations.
    Curated database of clinically interpreted genetic variants.

gene_burden
    Rare variant gene burden and collapsing studies.
    Aggregates rare variants across a gene to test for disease association.

genomics_england
    Genomics England PanelApp — expert-curated gene-disease panels from the 100,000 Genomes Project.

clingen
    ClinGen — curated gene-disease validity classifications based on systematic evidence review.

gene2phenotype
    Gene2Phenotype — curated database linking genes to developmental disorders and disease mechanisms.

orphanet
    Orphanet — rare disease gene associations from the European rare disease database.


SOMATIC MUTATION (cancer-relevant)
------------------------------------
eva_somatic
    ClinVar somatic variants — clinically interpreted somatic mutations linked to disease.

cancer_gene_census
    COSMIC Cancer Gene Census — manually curated list of genes with causal roles in cancer.

intogen
    IntOGen — computationally identified cancer driver genes from tumour sequencing data.

cancer_biomarkers
    Cancer Genome Interpreter (CGI) — cancer biomarkers linked to drug response or resistance.


AFFECTED PATHWAY
----------------
reactome
    Reactome — pathway annotations linking genes to biological processes and disease mechanisms.


LITERATURE
----------
europepmc
    Europe PMC text mining — automated extraction of gene-disease co-mentions from published literature.
    Scored by co-mention frequency and sentence context.

uniprot_literature
    UniProt curated literature — manually curated gene-disease associations from UniProt entries.


GENETIC LITERATURE
------------------
uniprot_variants
    UniProt manually curated variants — variant-level annotations from UniProt with disease links.


ANIMAL MODEL
------------
impc
    International Mouse Phenotyping Consortium (IMPC) — phenotypes from standardised mouse knockouts,
    mapped to human diseases.


RNA EXPRESSION
--------------
expression_atlas
    Expression Atlas — differential gene expression across tissues, cell types, and disease conditions.


CLINICAL
--------
clinical_precedence
    ChEMBL — drugs in clinical trials or approved for a given indication, linking drug targets to disease.


FUNCTIONAL GENOMICS
-------------------
crispr
    CRISPR viability screens (Project Score / DepMap) — genome-wide loss-of-function screens
    identifying essential genes in cancer cell lines.

crispr_screen
    Disease-specific CRISPR screens — targeted screens linking gene perturbations to disease-relevant phenotypes.
