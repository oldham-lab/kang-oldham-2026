BNIP3 — no direct AD PMID recalled (outer-mitochondrial-membrane mitophagy receptor; mitophagy impairment documented in AD, but BNIP3-specific AD PMID not confirmed)

CACNA1B — no direct AD PMID recalled (N-type voltage-gated Ca2+ channel; Ca2+ dyshomeostasis is a core AD mechanism, but N-type-specific AD PMID not confirmed)

CACNA2D1 — no direct AD PMID recalled (VGCC α2δ1 auxiliary subunit; Ca2+ signalling in AD context, but specific PMID not confirmed)
CLSTN1 — no direct AD PMID recalled (calsyntenin-1 regulates APP vesicle trafficking; AD-relevant function documented, but specific calsyntenin-1-AD PMID not confirmed)
DNM1 — no direct AD PMID recalled (dynamin-1; synaptic vesicle endocytosis; DNM1 reduction at AD synapses suggested, but specific PMID not confirmed; family member DNM1L is included)
DNM3 — no direct AD PMID recalled (dynamin-3; synaptic isoform; family member of DNM1L; specific AD PMID not confirmed)
DYNC1H1 — no direct AD PMID recalled (cytoplasmic dynein-1 heavy chain; retrograde axonal transport; DYNC1H1 mutations cause motor neuron disease; impaired retrograde transport implicated in AD, but specific DYNC1H1-AD PMID not confirmed)
HDAC2 — no direct AD PMID recalled (histone deacetylase 2; HDAC2 levels elevated in AD neurons and Hdac2 knockdown rescues synaptic plasticity in AD mice; omitted because independent PMID confirmation was not made at annotation time)
ITCH — no direct AD PMID recalled (NEDD4-family E3 ubiquitin ligase; proposed to ubiquitinate tau for proteasomal degradation; specific ITCH-AD PMID not confirmed)
LMTK2 — no direct AD PMID recalled (Lemur tail kinase 2; phosphorylates PP1 regulatory subunit, modulating tau phosphorylation; LMTK2 variants reported in AD genetics literature, but independent PMID not confirmed)
MADD — no direct AD PMID recalled (MAP kinase-activating death domain protein; DENN-domain Rab3-GEF; death domain signalling in neurodegeneration, but specific MADD-AD PMID not confirmed)
MAPK10 — no direct AD PMID recalled (JNK3; brain-enriched JNK isoform; phosphorylates tau; JNK family member of MAPK8 which is included; JNK3-specific AD PMID not confirmed independently)
MAPK14 — no direct AD PMID recalled (p38 MAPK; phosphorylates tau at several AD-relevant sites; p38 activated in AD neurofibrillary tangle-bearing neurons; independent PMID confirmation was not made at annotation time)
MAPK9 — no direct AD PMID recalled (JNK2; stress kinase; tau phosphorylation; JNK family member; specific JNK2-AD PMID not confirmed independently)
NTRK3 — no direct AD PMID recalled (TrkC; neurotrophin receptor; family member of NTRK2 included; TrkC-specific AD mechanism paper not confirmed independently)
NUMB — no direct AD PMID recalled (endocytic adaptor; Notch signalling; modulates γ-secretase/APP processing; AD-relevant function, but specific NUMB-AD PMID not confirmed)
PIK3C3 — no direct AD PMID recalled (VPS34 class III PI3K; generates PI3P to initiate autophagy; PI3P reduction impairs autophagy in AD, but specific VPS34/PIK3C3-AD PMID not confirmed)
PIK3R4 — no direct AD PMID recalled (VPS15; obligate VPS34-complex subunit; same reasoning as PIK3C3)
PIKFYVE — no direct AD PMID recalled (FYVE-domain PI kinase; synthesises PI(3,5)P2 at late endosomes; endolysosomal dysfunction in AD, but specific PIKFYVE-AD PMID not confirmed)
PPP2CA — no direct AD PMID recalled (PP2A catalytic subunit; major tau Ser/Thr phosphatase; PP2A activity is reduced in AD brain and PP2A reduction causes tau hyperphosphorylation; omitted because existing ref 11 covers calcineurin/PPP3CB and an independently confirmed PPP2CA-AD PMID was not recorded at annotation time)
PPP2R2B — no direct AD PMID recalled (PP2A regulatory B55β subunit; modulates PP2A substrate specificity toward tau; family relationship to PPP2CA above)
PPP2R2C — no direct AD PMID recalled (PP2A regulatory B55γ subunit; same reasoning as PPP2R2B)

PPP2R5E — no direct AD PMID recalled (PP2A regulatory B56ε subunit; same reasoning as PPP2R5D)

RAB1A — no direct AD PMID recalled (ER-to-Golgi COPII trafficking; early secretory pathway; indirect role in APP/tau glycosylation, but specific RAB1A-AD PMID not confirmed)
RAB11A — no direct AD PMID recalled (recycling endosome Rab; regulates APP recycling and potentially Aβ clearance; AD-relevant function suggested, but specific RAB11A-AD PMID not confirmed)
RAB3GAP1 — no direct AD PMID recalled (RAB3 GAP complex; synaptic vesicle release regulation; AD-adjacent function, but specific PMID not confirmed)
RAB3GAP2 — no direct AD PMID recalled (RAB3 GAP regulatory subunit; same reasoning as RAB3GAP1)
RAB7A — no direct AD PMID recalled (late endosomal Rab GTPase; marks enlarged late endosomes in AD; endosomal pathway gene, but specific RAB7A-AD mechanism PMID not confirmed independently)

UBE3A — no direct AD PMID recalled (Angelman syndrome HECT E3 ubiquitin ligase; proposed role in Aβ and tau ubiquitination; specific UBE3A-AD PMID not confirmed)

MAPK1 — indirect/speculative only (ERK2; activated in AD neurons; generic MAP kinase with hundreds of substrates; no AD-specific mechanistic link beyond broad stress-signalling pathway membership)
NTRK3 — no direct AD PMID recalled (see above; listed here also as near-miss neurotrophin family member)
PIK3CA — indirect/speculative only (class I PI3Kα; upstream of AKT/mTOR; AD connection entirely via the PTEN/AKT pathway included under PTEN; PIK3CA itself has no AD-specific mechanistic evidence)
SPTBN1 — indirect/speculative only (spectrin βII; emerging plasma biomarker of neurodegeneration; appears elevated in AD/TBI CSF but no direct AD mechanistic PMID available)
TRAPPC11 — indirect/speculative only (TRAPPII complex subunit; ER-to-Golgi tethering; APP and tau transit the early secretory pathway but a direct TRAPPC11-AD mechanism paper is not available)
