ITCH — no direct AD PMID recalled (NEDD4-family HECT E3 ubiquitin ligase; proposed to ubiquitinate tau for proteasomal degradation; specific ITCH-AD PMID not confirmed)
MAPK9 — no direct AD PMID recalled (JNK2; stress kinase; tau phosphorylation; JNK family member of MAPK8 which is included; specific JNK2-AD PMID not confirmed independently)
MAPK14 — no direct AD PMID recalled (p38 MAPK; phosphorylates tau at several AD-relevant sites and is activated in AD neurofibrillary tangle-bearing neurons; independent PMID confirmation was not made at annotation time)

PIK3C3 — no direct AD PMID recalled (VPS34 class III PI3K; generates PI3P to initiate autophagy; PI3P reduction impairs autophagy in AD, but specific VPS34/PIK3C3-AD PMID not confirmed)

PPP2R2B — no direct AD PMID recalled (PP2A regulatory B55β subunit; modulates PP2A substrate specificity toward tau; family relationship to PPP2CA; specific B55β-AD paper not confirmed)
PPP2R2C — no direct AD PMID recalled (PP2A regulatory B55γ subunit; same reasoning as PPP2R2B)
RIMS1 — no direct AD PMID recalled (active zone RIM1/RIMS1; synaptic vesicle priming scaffold; synaptic loss is an AD hallmark, but RIMS1-specific AD PMID not confirmed)
SNAP25 — no direct AD PMID recalled (synaptosomal-associated protein 25; presynaptic SNARE; SNAP25 reduced in AD neocortex at early Braak stages; specific AD-mechanism PMID not confirmed independently for this gene list)
STAM — no direct AD PMID recalled (signal transducing adaptor molecule; ESCRT-0 complex; early endosome-to-MVB sorting; endosomal pathway gene, but STAM-specific AD PMID not confirmed)
UBE3B — no direct AD PMID recalled (UBE3B HECT E3 ubiquitin ligase; synaptic protein turnover; UPS dysfunction in AD, but UBE3B-specific AD PMID not confirmed)
UBE4A — no direct AD PMID recalled (UFD2 homolog ubiquitin chain elongation E4 enzyme; E4-mediated polyubiquitin chain assembly; UPS in neurodegeneration, but UBE4A-specific AD PMID not confirmed)
UBE4B — no direct AD PMID recalled (UFD2 homolog ubiquitin E4 chain elongation enzyme; proposed role in ubiquitination of APP-related substrates; specific UBE4B-AD PMID not confirmed)
USP14 — no direct AD PMID recalled (ubiquitin-specific protease 14; proteasome-associated DUB; USP14 inhibition restores tau clearance in AD neurons; specific PMID confirmation was not made at annotation time for this gene list)
USP9X — no direct AD PMID recalled (deubiquitinase; stabilises synaptic scaffold proteins; proposed role in tau deubiquitination; specific USP9X-AD PMID not confirmed)
VPS13C — no direct AD PMID recalled (lipid transfer protein at ER-mitochondria and ER-endosome contacts; PARK23 Parkinson's disease gene; endosomal/mitochondrial dysfunction overlaps with AD, but specific VPS13C-AD PMID not confirmed)
