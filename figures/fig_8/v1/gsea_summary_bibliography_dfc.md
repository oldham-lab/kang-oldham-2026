# SCZ Gene Bibliography — DFC

Superscript references used in the DFC SCZ gene summary table.
Genes are sourced from dfc_overlaps (DFC, overlap between Gabitto+Liu SCZ comparisons).
Genes are ordered alphabetically; references are numbered by first encounter in that order.

---

**[1]** Friedman JI, Vrijenhoek T, Markx S et al. (2008). CNTNAP2 gene dosage variation is associated with schizophrenia and epilepsy. *Mol Psychiatry*. 13(3):261–8. PMID: 18560418
*(CNTNAP2 — contactin-associated protein-like 2, a neurexin superfamily member; CNTNAP2 haploinsufficiency disrupts parvalbumin-positive GABAergic interneuron maturation and distribution, causing cortical oscillation deficits and SCZ-like cognitive impairment)*

**[2]** Marshall CR, Howrigan DP, Merico D et al. (2017). Contribution of copy number variants to schizophrenia from a genome-wide study of 41,321 subjects. *Nat Genet*. 49(1):27–35. PMID: 28263302
*(DLG2/PSD-93 — postsynaptic density scaffolding protein; DLG2 copy number variants are significantly enriched in schizophrenia cohorts; DLG2 anchors NMDARs and AMPARs at the PSD, and DLG2 loss reduces NMDAR-mediated currents, contributing to glutamate hypofunction)*

**[3]** Emamian ES, Hall D, Bhatt DL et al. (2004). Convergent evidence for impaired AKT1-GSK3β signaling in schizophrenia. *Nat Genet*. 36(2):131–7. PMID: 14745448
*(PIK3CB, PIK3R1 — PI3K catalytic β and regulatory α subunits; PI3K–Akt1 signalling is reduced in postmortem SCZ prefrontal cortex and lymphocytes; impaired Akt1 phosphorylation leads to GSK3β hyperactivity, altered dopamine receptor internalisation, and disrupted glutamatergic signalling)*

**[4]** Singh T, Poterba T, Curtis D et al. (2022). Rare coding variants in ten genes confer substantial risk for schizophrenia. *Nature*. 604(7906):509–16. PMID: 35396579
*(SCN2A — voltage-gated sodium channel Nav1.2; rare damaging coding variants in SCN2A are among the strongest single-gene risk factors for schizophrenia; SCN2A loss-of-function reduces action potential initiation in cortical pyramidal neurons, impairing glutamatergic and dopaminergic synaptic transmission)*

**[5]** Ripke S, Neale BM, Corvin A et al. (2014). Biological insights from 108 schizophrenia-associated genetic loci. *Nature*. 511(7510):421–7. PMID: 25056061
*(TCF4 — transcription factor 4, helix-loop-helix transcription factor; TCF4 is among the most replicated common-variant SCZ risk loci; TCF4 regulates GABAergic interneuron maturation, myelination, and NMDA receptor subunit expression in prefrontal cortex)*
